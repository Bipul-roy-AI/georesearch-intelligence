Supported ranking formats

Use columns issn,year,system,category,quartile. An optional url column links to the source. For a SCImago CSV, enter its edition year below. Category quartiles and best quartiles have separate labels. The selected edition applies to all papers.

Import journal articles

Import RIS, PubMed NBIB, CSL or Zotero JSON, publisher RSS/Atom, or OAI-PMH Dublin Core XML. Saved journal records also appear in matching searches.
